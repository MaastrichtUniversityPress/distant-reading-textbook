### Task 5.3: Distant Reading in practice: feminist and postcolonial perspectives

When Franco Moretti coined the term distant reading, he proposed that we step back from individual texts to discern patterns across large corpora. Yet the corpora we build and the categories we apply are never neutral. Feminist and postcolonial scholars have shown that the archive itself reflects historical exclusions of gender, race, and language. As a homework, you will read texts that show how scholars using distant reading navigate these challenges:

- Barget, M., & Schreibman, S. (2025). Feminist DH: A historical perspective: Excavating the lives of women of the past. In L. M. Rhody & S. Schreibman (Eds.), Feminist digital humanities: Intersections in practice. University of Illinois Press. [https://www.bibliopen.org/9780252048302](https://www.bibliopen.org/9780252048302)

- Malate, E. (2025), Ideational Analysis and Integration of African Folktale in Science, Technology, and Education. Journal of the Digital Humanities Association of Southern Africa (DHASA), 6(2). [https://doi.org/10.55492/dhasa.v6i02.6748](https://doi.org/10.55492/dhasa.v6i02.6748)
