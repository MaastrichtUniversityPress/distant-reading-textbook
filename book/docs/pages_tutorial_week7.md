### Task 7.1: Homework Discussion – Data and AI Ethics (ca. 50 minutes)

In this session, we will discuss the data and AI ethics in research. As data-driven technologies become increasingly prevalent, it is essential to consider the social, legal, and moral responsibilities that come with their use.

For example, how can you ensure that your data collection and analysis respect individuals' privacy? How can you address bias in AI algorithms and ensure fairness in your results?

This session will also introduce you to frameworks and guidelines for ethical data and AI use. By the end of the discussion, you will have a better understanding of how to **conduct your research responsibly** and how to navigate the ethical challenges of working with data and AI.
