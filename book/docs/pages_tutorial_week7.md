### Task 7.1: Homework Discussion – Data and AI Ethics (ca. 50 minutes)

In this session, we will discuss the data and AI ethics in research. As data-driven technologies become increasingly prevalent, it is essential to consider the social, legal, and moral responsibilities that come with their use.

For example, how can you ensure that your data collection and analysis respect individuals' privacy? How can you address bias in AI algorithms and ensure fairness in your results?

This session will also introduce you to frameworks and guidelines for ethical data and AI use. By the end of the discussion, you will have a better understanding of how to **conduct your research responsibly** and how to navigate the ethical challenges of working with data and AI.


### Task 7.2: Course Evaluation and Q&A (ca. 50 minutes)

In this final session, we will reflect on the course as a whole and discuss your experiences, feedback, and suggestions for improvement. This is an opportunity to share what you have learned, what you found most valuable, and any challenges you encountered.

We will also address any **remaining questions** you may have about the course material, assignments, or future applications of the skills and concepts you have acquired. Through the anonymous IWIO evaluation, you will have a chance to provide constructive feedback that will help shape future iterations of the course.

This session is also an opportunity to celebrate your achievements and discuss how you can continue to develop your skills in digital humanities, data analysis, and media studies.
