# Week 7

## Task 7.1: Homework Discussion - Data and AI Ethics

We will discuss the readings on data and AI ethics given to you in week 6. Questions to discuss include the following:

- What are the social, legal and moral responsibilities that come with the increase of data-driven technologies in business and research?
- How can researchers / corporate data analysts ensure that their work respect individuals' privacy?
- How can researchers / corporate data analysts address bias in AI algorithms and ensure fairness in results gained using AI tools?
