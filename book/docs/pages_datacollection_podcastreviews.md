## Using podcast reviews as research sources

Podcast reviews are a popular source of data for media studies, cultural analysis, and digital humanities research. These user-generated texts provide reactions to audio content and offer insights into cultural trends. Some reviews are detailed and offer balanced critiques, while others may be extremely short, repetitive, or emotional. This is why working with podcast reviews can be challenging. It is also important to consider the metadata: Podcast reviews also often include (star) ratings, dates, or platform-specific tags that can help structure your research corpus and contextualise the content.

### Where to find podcast reviews

Reviews are accessible through platforms like Apple Podcasts, Spotify, or Google Podcasts. These platforms typically do not offer bulk downloads via the user interface, but several platforms offer an official API to collect the content automatically.  Always ensure that your data collection complies with platform terms of service and ethical guidelines, particularly regarding privacy and copyright. If you would like to try scraping podcast reviews from the Apple Store, you will find Python code in this handbook.
