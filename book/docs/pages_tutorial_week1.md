## Task 1.2: Investigative and Data-Driven Journalism

A field that has lately produced some interesting pieces that make use of (big) data analysis and visualization is **(investigative) data-driven journalism**. Data journalism is a practice that uses **digital resources, tools, and methods** to develop compelling narratives based on large amounts of data that are typically not comprehensible or accessible to a wide audience.

For this task, try to find your own examples of data-driven journalism and respond to the following questions. You will present your findings and discuss the examples you found in class in **Week 2**.

### Questions to Address

1. What data does the project use?
2. What modalities of communication does the project use?
3. What is the narrative that the visualization tries to convey?
4. Does it tell the story well?
